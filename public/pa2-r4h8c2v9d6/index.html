Plugin Alliance has one of the deepest catalogues in the audio industry. The app shows plugins and instruments as recognisable pictures. Click one and it opens on the selected channel strip. It works offline, and nothing leaves your Mac.

New in 2.0

What’s new in 2.0.

Version 2.0.0-preview.2, a trial preview. Here is what has changed since the first version.

Browse and search by brand

A Categories | Brands switch sits above the artwork. Categories shows Plugin Alliance’s own categories, and Brands shows every brand in the catalogue. Pick a brand and the grid narrows to it; combine it with a category, and click the brand again to clear it. Each tile shows its brand across the top and the plugin’s own name below, so far fewer names are cut off, and typing a brand name in search finds all of its plugins, even where the brand is not in the plugin’s name.

Shortcuts that work on any keyboard

The app sends function-key shortcuts to Flexi-Deck, which sit in the same place on every keyboard layout, whether it is German, French or British. The kit includes the matching Flexi-Deck project, BCD Launch Macros 2_0.

Plugins open in front

The app’s window fills the screen without macOS full screen, so a plugin window never opens behind it when Logic is full screen on another display.

Copy system info

One click in the Help tab puts your versions and settings on the clipboard, ready to paste into a message. It contains no plugin names, no files and nothing personal.

The Add form is in clear sections

In the evaluation build, the form is split into Module, Maker, Categories, Status and Artwork. The manufacturer’s own categories are blue and BCD’s shared categories are violet, each with a short key. The main action is a muted green.

Effect or Instrument, made clear (in the evaluation build)

Adding your own plugin uses a clear Effect | Instrument choice under the name. The Instrument switch, the manufacturer’s instrument category and BCD’s Instruments category follow each other, and choosing an instrument-type category turns Instrument on automatically.

Choose a brand when you add a plugin (in the evaluation build)

An optional Brand field suggests a brand from the name and only accepts brands already in the catalogue.

Easier to read

The tabs at the top of each drawer stay in place while the contents scroll, with more space between sections.

Faster inserts

On our test Macs, a tile click now gets the plugin into Logic about a second sooner than the first version.

A calmer first launch

The app asks for the main permission once, in front, a moment after the window appears, and takes you straight to the right Settings page, so the first click usually works. (macOS may still ask separately to let the app control Logic Pro the first time.)

Colour tags

Mark plugins with up to six colours you name yourself, and filter by one colour or several. Tag one plugin or a whole selection at once, and choose each colour from twelve muted shades in Prefs.

A Help tab

The version and build number, whether the permission it needs has been granted, what the app needs, and short fixes for the usual problems.

The header bar

Everything you need, along one bar.

Views, search and tools sit in the header. The Categories | Brands switch, the company row and the filters sit just beneath it. Here is what each one does.

01

The module count

Under the title, it shows how many modules match what you are looking at, out of the whole collection. It updates as you search and filter.

02

Grid, List and Sphere

Grid shows tiles with artwork. List shows rows sorted by plugin or company. Sphere is an endless drifting field for browsing rather than searching.

03

Dice and A–Z

The dice shuffles the order. In Sphere it reshuffles the field, and the ∞ button switches between an endless field and a single one, with each module once. In Grid and List, once a company or category is chosen, A–Z puts the tiles back in order.

04

Categories and Brands

A Categories | Brands switch sits above the artwork. Categories shows Plugin Alliance’s own categories. Brands shows every brand in the catalogue, such as Brainworx, SPL, Lindell Audio, Unfiltered Audio and elysia. Pick a brand and the grid narrows to it. Combine it with a category, and click the brand again to clear it. The two choices narrow each other, so you never click into an empty result.

05

Search and Logic Search

Type to filter modules by name. Type a brand name and you find all of its plugins, even where the brand is not in the plugin’s name, as with Apogee here. A small × appears at the end of the field to clear what you typed. The dot at the end opens Logic’s own plugin search.

06

Magnify

Switch it on and hover any module to see its artwork enlarged, with its name and company. It is switched off while you batch-select.

07

Batch

Click Batch, then click modules to select them. A bar opens at the bottom with Select all, favourite or unfavourite, hide or unhide, owned or not owned, colour tags, export, delete and Add to category. Delete removes your own additions. Done closes the bar.

08

+ Add

In the evaluation build, + Add adds a module by hand. The form is in clear sections: Module, Maker, Categories, Status and Artwork. Give the module a name, then the exact name Logic uses in its plugin menu, so it can be found. Under the name, choose Effect or Instrument. The optional Brand field suggests a brand from the name and only accepts brands already in the catalogue. Choose the manufacturer, then the categories: the manufacturer’s own are blue and BCD’s shared categories are violet. Mark it Owned or Hidden if you want to, add a colour tag, drop in artwork and press Add Module. The tabs at the top stay in place while the form scrolls.

If it is an instrument, Instrument must be selected. That triggers the Instrument macro, which creates the channel strip first.

09

Libraries

In the evaluation build, Libraries imports a library, or drop a .BCDLIB file anywhere in the window, or import a spreadsheet (CSV or XLSX). Every company has its own switch, so you can show or hide whole libraries. BCD Catalog and Your Additions show how many modules come from each.

10

Prefs

Choose whether the app opens in Grid, Sphere or where you left off, and set the tile size and artwork zoom. Pick a click glow and its colour, name your six colour tags, and choose whether tags show in List only or in Grid and Sphere too. The Sphere field can be endless or single.

11

Help

The Help tab shows the version and build number, so you can quote it when you send feedback. It also tells you whether the permission the app needs has been granted, with a button straight to the right Settings page. It lists what the app needs. Further down, Copy system info puts your versions and settings on the clipboard, with no plugin names, no files and nothing personal, to paste into a message to us. Below it are short fixes for the usual problems.

12

Quick filters: Favs, Owned, Hidden and colours

Quick filters that combine with the category and company rows. Favs shows what you have hearted, Owned shows what you own, and Hidden shows what you have put away. The six colour dots filter by colour tag.

13

Colour tags

Mark any plugin with one of six colours, and name each colour for whatever suits you: a project, a sound, plugins to try. The tags are yours. They sit on top of Plugin Alliance’s own categories and never change them.

Tagging. In List view, shift-click a tile to open its colour picker: the six dots tag the tile, and the × clears the tag. The tile takes the tint. On Grid and Sphere tiles a small dot opens the same picker; in Prefs you can limit tags to List. To tag many at once, select them with Batch and click a colour on the bar. In the evaluation build you can also choose a tag when you add or edit a module.

Finding. The six dots in the header filter by colour. Click one, or several to see them together. Hover a dot to see its name and how many plugins carry it; a colour nothing is tagged with is dimmed. The colour filter works together with search, company, category and the quick filters.

In Prefs. Name the six colours and choose each one from a set of twelve muted shades. A shade another tag already uses is greyed out, so no two tags look alike. Then choose whether tags show in List only, or in Grid and Sphere too.

14

Categories and companies

Filter by company, then by category. Just under Magnify, a circle at the end of the category row shows or hides all the categories, and a circle on the row below shows or hides the companies.

15

Module cards

Each tile shows its brand across the top and the plugin’s own name below, so far fewer names are cut off. Click the artwork or the name and the plugin opens on the selected channel strip in Logic. The dot marks owned or not owned, the pencil edits the module, and the heart adds it to your favourites. Grid tiles show the artwork, List tiles show the name.

System requirements

DAW: Logic Pro 11.1 or later (required for the plugin search the app uses).

Flexi-Deck: the free Flexi-Deck Player, running, with the BCD Launch Macros 2_0 project loaded (the 2_0 project is in the kit). The app hands each plugin name to Logic through these macros. Without them you can still browse, but a click won’t open the plugin.

Mac: Apple Silicon, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later.
