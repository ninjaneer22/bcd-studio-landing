The header bar

Everything you need, along one bar.

Views, search and tools sit in the header. Categories, companies and filters sit just beneath it. Here is what each one does.

01

The module count

Under the title, it shows how many modules match what you are looking at, out of the whole collection. It updates as you search and filter.

02

Grid, List and Sphere

Grid shows tiles with artwork. List shows rows sorted by plugin or company. Sphere is an endless drifting field for browsing rather than searching.

03

Dice and A–Z

The dice shuffles the order. In Sphere it reshuffles the field, and the ∞ button switches between an endless field and a single one, with each module once. In Grid and List, once a company or category is chosen, A–Z puts the tiles back in order.

04

Search and Logic Search

Type to filter modules by name. The dot at the end of the field opens Logic’s own plugin search.

05

Magnify

Switch it on and hover any module to see its artwork enlarged, with its name and company. It is switched off while you batch-select.

06

Batch

Click Batch, then click modules to select them. A bar opens at the bottom with Select all, favourite or unfavourite, hide or unhide, owned or not owned, colour tags, export, delete and Add to category. Delete removes your own additions. Modules that ship with Omni are hidden instead, and ⌘Z undoes it. Done closes the bar.

07

+ Add

Add a module by hand. Give it a name, then the exact name Logic uses in its plugin menu, so it can be found. Choose the manufacturer and categories, mark it Instrument, Owned or Hidden, add a colour tag, drop in artwork and press Add Module.

08

Libraries

Import a library, or drop a .BCDLIB file anywhere in the window, or import a spreadsheet (CSV or XLSX). Every company has its own switch, so you can show or hide whole libraries. BCD Catalog and Your Additions show how many modules come from each.

09

Prefs

Choose whether Omni opens in Grid, Sphere or where you left off, and set the tile size and artwork zoom. Pick a click glow and its colour, name your six colour tags, and choose whether tags show in List only or in Grid and Sphere too. The Sphere field can be endless or single.

10

Favs, Owned and Hidden

Three filters that combine with the category and company rows. Favs shows what you have hearted, Owned shows what you own, and Hidden shows what you have put away. Colour dots filter by tag.

11

Categories and companies

Filter by company, then by that company’s own categories. With everything showing, the same row becomes BCD’s 14 categories, so every manufacturer shares one vocabulary. The circle at the end of the category row shows or hides all the categories. The circle at the end of the company row shows or hides the companies.

12

Module cards

Click the artwork or the name and the plugin opens on the selected channel strip in Logic. The dot marks owned or not owned, the pencil edits the module, and the heart adds it to your favourites. Grid tiles show the artwork, List tiles show the name.
