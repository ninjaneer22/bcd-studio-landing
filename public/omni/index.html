New in 2.2.0

What’s new in 2.2.

Version 2.2.0, 13 October 2026. Here is what has changed since the 17 August beta. Omni Essentials uses the same shortcuts and the same Flexi-Deck project.

Shortcuts that work on any keyboard

Omni now sends function-key shortcuts to Flexi-Deck, which sit in the same place on every keyboard layout, whether it is German, French or British. This needs the updated Flexi-Deck project, BCD Launch Macros 2_0. Close the older BCD Launch Macros project in Flexi-Deck and open the 2_0 one.

Plugins open in front, on every display

Omni’s green window button now makes the window fill the screen without macOS full screen, so a plugin window never opens behind Omni when Logic is full screen on another display.

Send us your details

Help has a Copy system info button. One click puts your versions and settings on the clipboard, ready to paste into a message. It contains no plugin names, no files and nothing personal.

Adding an instrument keeps everything in step

The Instrument switch, the manufacturer’s own instrument category (for example V - Instruments) and BCD’s Instruments category now follow each other, in both directions. Your own categories are never touched.

Easier to read

The tabs at the top of every drawer stay in place while the contents scroll. There is more space between sections, friendlier text, a firmer outline around fields and buttons, and less blur behind the drawers.

The Add form is in clear sections

Module, Maker, Categories, Status and Artwork. The manufacturer’s own categories are blue and BCD’s shared categories are violet, each with a short key. The main actions (Add Module, Import library, Done in Batch) are in a muted green.

Give Omni a screen of its own

Swipe up with three or four fingers (or press Control and Up Arrow) to open Mission Control. Click the + at the top right to add a new Space, then drag Omni’s window onto it. Swipe left or right with three or four fingers to move between Logic and Omni.

Earlier in this cycle

Pick your accent colour

Prefs, Display: six swatches change the highlight colour everywhere. Omni MAX stays orange unless you choose another.

Faster inserts

Clicking a tile gets Logic’s search and the plugin on screen about a second sooner than the August beta.

A calmer first launch

The app asks for the main permission once, in front, a moment after the window appears, and takes you straight to the right Settings page. No hidden pop-up behind the splash.

Which version do you have?

The About window and the Help tab show the version and build number, so you can quote it when you send feedback.

A softer, calmer look

A very faint light across the top of the window and a hint of shade at the bottom, easier on the eye over a long session. It is always on and there is nothing to set.

The header bar

Everything you need, along one bar.

Views, search and tools sit in the header. Categories, companies and filters sit just beneath it. Here is what each one does.

01

The module count

Under the title, it shows how many modules match what you are looking at, out of the whole collection. It updates as you search and filter.

02

Grid, List and Sphere

Grid shows tiles with artwork. List shows rows sorted by plugin or company. Sphere is an endless drifting field for browsing rather than searching.

03

Dice and A–Z

The dice shuffles the order. In Sphere it reshuffles the field, and the ∞ button switches between an endless field and a single one, with each module once. In Grid and List the dice shuffles the tiles, and A–Z puts them back in order. Click A–Z again to flip to Z–A.

04

Search and Logic Search

Type to filter modules by name. A small × appears at the end of the field to clear what you typed. The dot at the end opens Logic’s own plugin search.

05

Magnify

Switch it on and hover any module to see its artwork enlarged, with its name and company. It is switched off while you batch-select.

06

Batch

Click Batch, then click modules to select them. A bar opens at the bottom with Select all, favourite or unfavourite, hide or unhide, owned or not owned, colour tags, export, delete and Add to category. Delete removes your own additions. Done closes the bar.

07

+ Add

Add a module by hand. The form is in clear sections: Module, Maker, Categories, Status and Artwork. Give the module a name, choose Effect or Instrument, then enter the exact name Logic uses in its plugin menu, so it can be found. Choose the manufacturer, then the categories. The manufacturer’s own categories are blue and BCD’s shared categories are violet. Mark it Owned or Hidden if you want to, add a colour tag, drop in artwork and press Add Module. The form scrolls, and the tabs at the top stay in place.

If it is an instrument, choose Instrument in the Effect | Instrument pair under the name. Picking an instrument category sets it for you. Instruments get their channel strip created first.

08

Libraries

Import a library, or drop a .BCDLIB file anywhere in the window, or import a spreadsheet (CSV or XLSX). Every company has its own switch, so you can show or hide whole libraries. BCD Catalog and Your Additions show how many modules come from each.

09

Prefs

Choose whether Omni opens in Grid, Sphere or where you left off, and set the tile size and artwork zoom. Pick an accent colour from six swatches and the highlight colour changes throughout. Choose a click glow and whether it uses your accent or white, name your six colour tags, and choose whether tags show in List only or in Grid and Sphere too. The Sphere field can be endless or single.

10

Help

The Help tab shows the version and build number of your copy, whether the permission Omni needs is switched on (with a button straight to the right page in System Settings), what Omni needs, and a tip for giving Omni a screen of its own. Further down, Copy system info puts your versions and settings on the clipboard, with no plugin names, no files and nothing personal, to paste into a message to us. Below it are short fixes for the usual problems.

11

Quick filters: Favs, Owned, Hidden and colours

Quick filters that combine with the category and company rows. Favs shows what you have hearted, Owned shows what you own, and Hidden shows what you have put away. The six colour dots filter by colour tag.

12

Colour tags

Mark any plugin with one of six colours, and name each colour for whatever suits you: a project, a sound, plugins to try. The tags are yours. They sit on top of the categories and never change them.

Tagging. In List view, shift-click a tile to open its colour picker: the six dots tag the tile, and the × clears the tag. The tile takes the tint. On Grid and Sphere tiles a small dot opens the same picker; in Prefs you can limit tags to List. To tag many at once, select them with Batch and click a colour on the bar. You can also choose a tag when you add or edit a module.

Finding. The six dots in the header filter by colour. Click one, or several to see them together. Hover a dot to see its name and how many plugins carry it; a colour nothing is tagged with is dimmed. The colour filter works together with search, company, category and the quick filters.

In Prefs. Name the six colours and choose each one from a set of twelve muted shades. A shade another tag already uses is greyed out, so no two tags look alike. Then choose whether tags show in List only, or in Grid and Sphere too.

13

Categories and companies

Filter by company, then by that company’s own categories. With everything showing, the same row becomes BCD’s 14 categories, so every manufacturer shares one vocabulary. Just under Magnify, a circle at the end of the category row shows or hides all the categories, and a circle on the row below shows or hides the companies.

14

Module cards

Click the artwork or the name and the plugin opens on the selected channel strip in Logic. The dot marks owned or not owned, the pencil edits the module, and the heart adds it to your favourites. Grid tiles show the artwork, List tiles show the name.

System requirements.

DAW: Logic Pro 11.1 or later (required for the plugin search Omni uses).

Flexi-Deck: the free Flexi-Deck Player, running, with the BCD Launch Macros 2_0 project loaded. Omni hands each plugin name to Logic through these macros. Without them you can still browse, but a click won’t open the plugin.

Mac: Apple Silicon, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later.
